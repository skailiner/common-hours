# Put the right people in the right hours.

Turn your team's availability and skills into a draft rota. Review staffing gaps, workloads and fixed assignments, then download a readable brief to discuss with the people involved.

## Who it is for

Volunteer coordinators planning small, single-day events.

## Get a useful first result

1. Try the repair-cafe example or choose Start my event.
2. Set equal time blocks, add skill labels and people, then specify roles and headcounts.
3. Create a draft plan. Inspect any unfilled role and discuss the actual constraints before changing them.
4. Download the team brief for discussion and save a roster JSON file for reopening and editing.

## Know the boundary

Nothing is autosaved. A fixed assignment is not confirmed consent; a fully filled role is not a safety assessment. Check qualifications, breaks, fatigue, supervision and applicable requirements separately. The brief has no calendar date or time zone. Share personal data only with permission.
